# Ticket pack #2 - Home

## Ticket 1: See offers on Home

**User story**
- As a customer, I want to see new products and offers on Home, so that I know what is new.

**Acceptance criteria**
- AC1 Given I am signed in, When I open Home,
  Then I see a banner carousel of new products and offers.
- AC2 Given I am on Home, When I move to the next banner (swipe or tap a dot),
  Then the next banner shows.
- AC3 Given I am on Home, When I tap a banner,
  Then nothing happens, because banners are display only.

**Scope:** Display-only banner carousel on Home, with dots to move between banners. The "Order now" button sits below the dots.

**Non-scope:** managing banners (Admin web app); tappable banners or links; personal recommendations

## Ticket 2: Jump to the menu from Home

**User story**
- As a customer, I want quick buttons on Home, so that I reach the menu in one tap.

**Acceptance criteria**
- AC1 Given I am on Home, When I look at the quick icons,
  Then I see 4: Best-Sellers, Cups, Cones, Take-away Tubs.
- AC2 Given I am on Home, When I tap the "Cups" icon,
  Then the menu opens with the Cups category selected.
- AC3 Given I am on Home, When I tap "Order Now",
  Then the menu opens on the All category.

**Scope:** 4 quick icons and the "Order Now" button.

**Non-scope:** the menu itself (Menu pack)
